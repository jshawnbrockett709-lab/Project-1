About the show

THE YARD

A comedy podcast that launched in 2021 and features Ludwig, Nick, Slime, and Aiden.

What is The Yard?

The Yard is a long-form comedy and conversation podcast centered on four hosts. Episodes cover internet culture, creator life, games, personal stories, current happenings, and plenty of conversations that take unexpected turns.

The show is available as a podcast and also has video content for viewers who want to watch the conversation.

“Four friends, one table, and a lot to talk about.”
